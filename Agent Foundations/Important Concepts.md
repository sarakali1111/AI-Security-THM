**MITRE ATLAS** (Adversarial Threat Landscape for Artificial-Intelligence Systems) is a knowledge base of adversarial tactics and techniques targeting machine learning systems. Modelled on the ATT&CK framework, it documents real-world and research-backed attacks across the AI attack lifecycle, from initial access and model theft to inference manipulation and data poisoning. Each entry is grounded in observed incidents or proof-of-concept research. Security teams use ATLAS to identify AI-specific attack surfaces, select defensive controls, and communicate AI risk in a structured vocabulary. It complements the OWASP LLM Top 10 by providing technique-level depth rather than vulnerability-category summaries.
